§2

The symbol should be the totals rule

Recommended · 32×32 grid

Two unequal blocks standing on one rule that is neither longer nor shorter than the parts it carries. That is the product's entire claim, drawn literally — and it is the same shape as the ratio rail and the totals rule under the breakdown table, so the mark is the interface's own vocabulary rather than an ornament.
